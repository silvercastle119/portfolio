Bar & Pub Revitalization

Gen Z Insight, Participation-Driven Activation, and Budgeted Execution

Date

Sep – Dec 2022

Role

Project Lead & Planner

Tools

Desk Research, Interviews, Microsoft Presentation, Budget Planning

Category

Local Marketing, Brand Repositioning, Experiential Activation

Overview

This project focused on rebuilding the relationship between a local bar, Juyuso, and Gen Z customers after COVID-19 by repositioning the brand and designing a participation-driven experience.

Rather than relying on traditional promotions, I approached the problem as a visit-conversion challenge—how to turn awareness into actual visits through on-site participation.

After being selected as the 1st-place pitch team, we moved beyond a proposal and executed the project in-market, where I led strategy, creative direction, and operational planning.

As a result, the project contributed to an estimated 20% increase in Gen Z visits and sales.

Challenge

After COVID-19, Gen Z drinking behavior shifted toward smaller-group, experience-driven occasions, making traditional bar promotions less effective.

Juyuso had strong brand elements but lacked a clear reason for Gen Z to visit and participate. Awareness alone was not translating into actual foot traffic.

The core challenge was to define a simple, compelling value proposition and connect it to an on-site experience that drives real participation and repeat visits.

Insight → Idea

Insight

Gen Z does not visit places just because they exist—they visit when there is something to do.

Drinking is increasingly about shared experiences and participation, not just consumption.

Without a participation mechanism, awareness rarely converts into real visits.

↓

Idea

Turn the venue into a participatory experience that people actively engage with, not just passively visit.

Strategy

Participation-Driven Experience Design

I reframed the venue not as a place to visit, but as a place to participate, designing a simple and repeatable interaction that encourages on-site engagement.

Gen Z Behavior-Based Positioning

I aligned the concept with Gen Z’s shift toward small-group, experience-oriented occasions, ensuring the activation felt socially relevant and easy to join.

Feasibility-First Planning

I built a realistic budget and operational plan, prioritizing key elements to ensure the experience could be executed effectively within constraints.
