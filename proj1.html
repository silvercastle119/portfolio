KT Y Branded Content

Gen Z Content Creation & Brand Communication for YouTube & Instagram

Date

Mar – Jul 2025

Role

Content Creator & Marketer

Tools

Premiere Pro, Instagram for Business, YouTube Analytics

Category

Branded Content, Social Media

Overview

I produced branded content for KT’s Gen Z brand, ‘Y’ across YouTube and Instagram, focusing on creating platform-native content that balances entertainment and brand communication.

Rather than simply producing videos, I structured content to maximize early retention and integrate brand messages within relatable everyday scenarios. As a result, Instagram Reels reached up to 121K views, and YouTube videos consistently achieved 3K+ views per upload.

I also led coordination with in-house practitioners by developing video briefs and PPMs, collecting feedback, and refining scripts and on-screen messaging—especially for time-sensitive event content where clarity and engagement were both critical.

Challenge

KT needed to strengthen awareness and relevance for its Gen Z brand in a saturated telecom market.

The core challenge was not just visibility, but how to communicate brand value without triggering ad fatigue. Content needed to feel native, engaging, and relatable, while still delivering clear and accurate information—especially for time-sensitive event promotions.

Insight → Idea

Insight

Gen Z audiences tend to disengage from content that feels like advertising, but actively engage with content that reflects their everyday experiences.

This means brand messages are more effective when discovered naturally, rather than delivered upfront.

↓

Idea

Make the brand appear naturally within relatable everyday moments, instead of presenting it as an advertisement.

Strategy

Platform-Native Content Design

I designed content specifically for each platform by applying short-form storytelling principles such as strong 3-second hooks, fast pacing, and concise captions to improve early retention and completion rates.

Relatable Scenario-Based Storytelling

Instead of leading with product messaging, I built narratives around Gen Z’s everyday situations (e.g., living alone, campus life) and embedded the brand message within those moments to increase authenticity and engagement.

Information Clarity without Breaking Flow

For event-driven content, I structured messaging to deliver key information clearly while maintaining narrative flow, ensuring both accuracy and watchability.

Execution

Communication & Optimization

  • Led coordination with in-house practitioners through video briefs and PPMs
  • Refined scripts, captions, and on-screen text to balance brand accuracy and content engagement
  • Adjusted messaging structure based on feedback to improve clarity and flow

Performance-Based Iteration

  • Tracked content performance metrics including views and engagement
  • Identified which elements improved retention (hook, pacing, messaging clarity)
  • Applied these learnings to continuously improve subsequent content
